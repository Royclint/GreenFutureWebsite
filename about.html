About Mpumalanga GreenFuture

Learn more about our history, mission, vision, and the communities we serve.

Our History

Established in 2018 in Mbombela, GreenFuture began as a small clean‑up initiative along the Crocodile River. What started as a grassroots effort quickly grew into a registered non‑profit organisation. Today, we work with schools, residents, eco‑volunteers, donors, and corporate sponsors to promote sustainability across the Lowveld.

Our journey reflects the power of community action — from weekend clean‑ups to large‑scale tree‑planting campaigns, GreenFuture has become a trusted name in environmental protection in Mpumalanga.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable living and protect ecosystems through education, clean‑up campaigns, and tree‑planting initiatives. We empower communities to take ownership of their environment and inspire future generations to value and safeguard nature.

Our Vision

A greener Mbombela where communities actively protect the environment and future generations inherit thriving ecosystems. We envision a future where sustainability is part of everyday life, supported by schools, businesses, and residents working together.

Our Purpose

GreenFuture exists to act as a unifying platform for environmental action in the Lowveld. Our website consolidates campaigns, resources, and opportunities for involvement into one professional hub, replacing fragmented social media posts with a credible and accessible source.

Our Audience

We serve a diverse audience across Mbombela:

  • Schools: Teachers and learners integrating sustainability into education.
  • Residents: Community members joining clean‑ups and tree‑planting drives.
  • Eco‑volunteers: Individuals passionate about environmental activism.
  • Donors: Supporters providing financial or material contributions.
  • Corporate sponsors: Businesses demonstrating corporate social responsibility.
